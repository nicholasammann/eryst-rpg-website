# Traits

Traits are categorized as:
  - Natural Traits
  - Event Traits

Traits are how a character can:
  - Explain an inherent ability, physical feature, or mental feature
  - Undergo a sudden physical or mental change

## Natural Traits

| Name                   | Description                                                                                                                                                 | Notes |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----- |
| Strong Ancestors       | Add +2 to your Skill Roll when exerting your strength to achieve your goal.                                                                                 | ---   |
| Brawny Ancestors       | Add +2 to your Skill Roll when enduring something physically strenuous.                                                                                     | ---   |
| Nimble Ancestors       | Add +2 to your Skill Roll when attempting to do something quickly or with fancy footwork.                                                                   | ---   |
| Clever Ancestors       | Add +2 to your Skill Roll when trying to figure out a puzzle or learn something new.                                                                        | ---   |
| Wise Ancestors         | Add +2 to your Skill Roll when deciding on the best course of action.                                                                                       | ---   |
| Natural Weapon         | You have claws, long teeth, spikes, or another physical feature that can be used as an improvised weapon. Unarmed attacks deal 1d4 damage.                  | ---   |
| Protective Exterior    | You have scales, tough skin, carapace, or a similar defensive layer. Reduce any physical damage taken by 1 point.                                           | ---   |
| Winged Ancestors       | You have wings. You gain the ability to fly. Your wings must remain uncovered to enable flight. Decrease the damage reduction effect from armor by 1 point. | ---   |
| Underground Ancestors  | You gain the ability to see normally in semi-darkness.                                                                                                      | ---   |
| Keen Perception        | You have exceptional eyesight and hearing. You gain the Novice rank in Perception.                                                                          | ---   |
| Skilled Climber        | You are a natural climber. You gain the Novice rank in Climbing.                                                                                            | ---   |
| Born Swimmer           | You are a natural swimmer. You gain the Novice rank in Swimming.                                                                                            | ---   |
| Natural Skill Affinity | Gain the Novice rank in any Physical skill.                                                                                                                 | ---   |
