3 actions per turn

Each action is 2 seconds

Each turn is 6 seconds

Move
  - X distance per 1 action
Weapon Attack
  - 1 attack per X actions, depending on weapon
    ○ Light weapon: 1 attack costs 1 action
    ○ Medium weapon: 1 attack costs 1 actions
    ○ Heavy weapon: 1 attack costs 2 actions
Cast Spell
Encourage ally
Distract enemy
