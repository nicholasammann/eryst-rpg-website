# Plot

1. Hear whale song in response to otter blowing ritual horn
2. Feel a sense of awe when are first entering the lagoon
3. Start to feel something is wrong as they traverse deeper into the ocean
4. Reach the temple - sense of mystery
5. Sharks trapped in the temple other than final room, whales trapped them there to prevent them from attacking the creatures in the bay
6. Whale is sick and cannot sing, needs an item to heal. Whales are too big to reach the room where the Moonwave flowers are, because the ceiling collapsed (introduce potential villain for future? The octopus? Whales don't even know it was the octopus but party finds a clue).
7. Party retrieves the item, heal the whale. Sharks calm down. Party can return to Holt Baleen.


## Scene 1

The deep note of the baleen horn echoes across the water. Melody the otter stands calmly on the shore next to all of you, eyes closed, waiting.

The sand beneath your paws begins to bounce and shake, and the vibrating beach transfers a booming sound directly through the bones of your footpaws. Your bodies reverberate with the power of it, making the first notes of the horn seem like an echo that came too soon.

As the ground quiets, you notice that the air tastes dry in your mouth. The otter points out to the center of the bay and says, "Quickly, into the water! Dive! Don't keep them waiting!"

You throw yourselves into the shallow waves, forgetting in the confusion to close your mouths... then finding that you don't need to. The saltwater tastes cool and clean, and oxygen fills your lungs.

Melody's face appears before you all, and she holds a paw out, pointing into deeper water, into the swirl of kelp and colors.

You see her feet leave the shallows, leaving small pawprints that are quickly smoothed away by the current.


## Scene 2

All around you, fish dart in and out of the sea flora. The constant movement of diverse ocean life gives you a feeling of impermanence.

Even the sunlight, which should be dimmer, permeates the water around you and gives the very space you swim through a cosmological glow.


## Scene 3

As you travel beyond the shallows and into deeper water, the light around you darkens to a colder blue. The fish around you seem sluggish, almost drifting with the current rather than swimming. You watch the deeper current twist and tangle thick fronds of kelp into snarls, no longer free to spread out. The ocean life becomes more sparse and you can see farther ahead than you could near the shore. In the distance, you see a large craggy structure rising up from the ocean floor.


## Scene 4

You approach the structure and a massive set of algae-covered doors rises up before you. At the top of the arch, a large gemstone sparkles, partially catching the rays of lights that have pierced the upper waters and made it down here. As the light shifts, the color of the gemstone changes too, at times deciding to display a deep fire or emerald shade, but just as often putting forth a dazzling rainbow of hues. One beam of light in particular outshines any others, illuminating a small circle on the sandy ocean floor about 10 feet in front of the doors. There are no levers or handles that you can see.
