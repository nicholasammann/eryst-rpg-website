The party needs to talk to the whale. the whale will tell them to get the flower in the far room. the party will use this flower to heal second whale. once the second wheel is healed, the two whales are able to sing together and restore balance to the surrounding area. the shark in the temple is calmed down and the party is able to return to Holt baleen with the flower that they need. from Holt baleen they will follow the currents North  and will pass by the volcano.

As you turn the corner of the hallway, you see a large set of green and gold doors Rising many feet and reaching a ceiling far above. on each door, set just within your reach, are large golden Door knockers.  at the base of each ring is the shape of a whale's tail curved and pointing towards the door.

The doors swing open slowly  and you feel a slow, powerful pull as  the movement of the door is creates a current in the room. Through the doors you see a grand chamber  several hundred feet wide  and with a high ceiling. in the center of the  chamber float  two enormous whales  Side by side. Each has a mottled blue back and fins speckled with patches of white.  one is  cream colored underneath and crusted with barnacles. The second whale has a light blue belly with a pattern of spiraling, swirling ridges.

“Children of above, you have come. Witness my partner who is sick and fading. Witness the cure, close but unreachable to us. Restore them, and restore the balance of our song.

In this place there is a flower. Witness the petals colored as the ocean spray. Return with one.”

 to the north lies a sharp craggy Mountain rising from a small island  with barely enough room to dock your boat. smoke pours out of the deep crevice in the top of the volcano. no life can be found on the island, having long been smothered by Ash and lava flows. on the western edge of the island you see a small Cove with just enough of Backwater that you could safely moor Your Vessel.

Salamander in the cave at the base of the volcano.
