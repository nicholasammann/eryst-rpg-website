# Plot

## Green River Enclave
- Pine needle thatching
- Butterfly message tower
- Sawmill with steam room for bending wood for boats and ships
- Fish nets
- Herb library

## Tasks
- Greeted by Mayor Brambleberry
  - Brings you to her home for the night, food and lodging
- Talk with Estevot (herb expert)
  - Says the flower only grows in the Swiftcurrent Islands, need to sail there to pick it
- Find Sergeant Marigold (ship captain) in Moonspruce Forest
  - Fight giant spiders (2)
  - Carry Marigold back to town for healing (spider bite)

Sergeant Marigold is an average-sized and lanky otter. She is the captain of the ship Huckleberry. She is sad and not confident when she is sober. When she drinks, she becomes very confident and even a bit pushy.

Estevot is a little and thickset mole. He understands lizard talk. Estevot has a strained voice. He is exceptional at healing beasts, but he is especially awkward interacting with others. Love is what drives Estevot. He does his best for his friend's sake. He is an herbalist and healher in Green River Enclave.
