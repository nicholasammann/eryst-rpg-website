# Characters

## Lady Joanna

Ever since the tragic death of Lord Bernard's father, Lady Joanna has adopted the mindset that Bernard (and all other ravens in the western tower) need to be tough and self sufficient.

Any time Bernard asks for help, she will tell him that he needs to figure it out himself. This is a misguided attempt to toughen him up for his own good.

Lord Bernard's father was kind and gentle and Lady Joanna attributes his death to his gentle personality. She talks down about him sometimes but in truth she talks like that to mask her grief about his death, and missing him.
