### Approaching the Mountains
- Lord Bernard shows signs of doubt in his willingness to ask the party for help
(feelings that are small example of later plot theme)

You exit the trees and the space around you opens up into a meadow of tall grass and late summer flowers.

Ahead, the foothills of the mountains rise steeply, sharp ridgelines angling into steep slopes of loose rock and craggy cliffs.

Your current path winds between two such ridges. In the distance you see a thin line of switchbacks cut into the mountain side, zig-zagging back and forth before disappearing over the top of the rise.

It will be a tiring hike to reach the mountains proper.

Lord Bernard: "Hmm, the path looks... difficult."

The crumbling cliff edge looks unshaped, contrasting with where the flat pathway meets the weathered mountainside.

Perhaps some of you wonder how long ago this footpath was hewn into the mountains, and by who.

The answer may lie somewhere in the extensive libraries at the University in Rosenrigg, or it may be lost to time.

As you round a bend in the path, you see a sparkle of movement up the path. Ahead, the yellow-white of old bone stands out from the dark gray stone of the path, and you make out the skeleton of a long-dead traveler slumped against the wall of the mountainside. Sunlight reflects off of an amulet still hanging around the skeleton's neck.

Three large snowflakes hang suspended in the air nearby, drifting slightly with the wind.

#### Ice Spirits

Three floating snowflakes bound to a magical artifact. They obey the commands of the wielder of the artifact.

They function similar to mage hand, but with some special abilities. 

If a snowflake loses all of its health or melts (takes damage from heat), it is permanently gone.

#### Magical Amulet
