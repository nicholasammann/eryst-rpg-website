## Character Engagement Hooks

### Sagittarius
- Astronomy
  - Tie in the constellations and planets to the plot
  - Have NPCs talk about/interested in astronomy
- Professor
  - Teachable moments for younger NPCs (Finnan?)
  - Moments where he can play the role of teacher

### Friar Alf
- Sol (sun goddess)
  - Preach about Sol to people
  - Perform some rite or practice to praise Sol
  - Anything sun related
- Tortle
  - Opportunities to retreat into his shell
