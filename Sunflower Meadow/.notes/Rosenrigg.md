# Rosenrigg

## Upper Rim
 the richest area of the city. mostly residential. relatively vertical buildings with decorative elements and landscape surroundings. only about 20 to 50 houses are in the upper room.

## Lower Rim
 the  businesses and middle class living area of the city. residential neighborhoods and cafes, shops, and entertainment services or make up the bulk of buildings here. residents from the upper Rim frequent the business is here.

## The Slopes
 the seedier, poorer area of the city. literally on the steeper ground below the rim of the crater, buildings here are smaller, more closely positioned to each other, and prone to collapsing occasionally. there are shops, cheap housing, and speakeasies. drinking is prohibited in the slopes due to safety concerns because of the terrain, but this is a farce for the purpose of restricting alcohol sales to those who can afford to run a business in the lower rim.

## University Grounds
 a walled off area of Rosen rig containing the buildings owned by the University. only faculty,  students, and registered visitors are allowed inside school grounds.

The University was founded by the dragon Obsiduin, who lives deep within the volcano. guided by his main Pursuits of knowledge and wealth, he orchestrated the beginning of the University as a means of nurturing research and education such that he could gain the knowledge discovered by University scholars. in addition to Desiring the knowledge resulting from the school's research, he profits from the school and town financially. he has a horde of wealth that he funds using taxes from the town and profits from University pursuits, but that he also uses to fund the university if he judges that there will be knowledge gained from his investment. he may provide the funding for a particularly expensive experiment, or even cover the tuition of an especially promising student.


Obsiduin's brother Ninguin lives in the mountains to the north the crown of Ninguin though he hasn't been seen in many centuries, so his existence is largely unknown.

Obsiduin's existence is only known by the Headmaster and previous Headmasters any communication with them to do and must go through the current Headmaster. a tunnel to Obsiduin's Lair exists in the basement of the Headmasters quarters. Publicly, the school is known to have a wealthy but secret of donor.


## Plot
The darkness that took over the land is caused by some magical issue related to Obsiduin that only Ninguin can fix. it may be related to the relationship between  Obsiduin and Ninguin. the theme of the story should be healing the brotherly bond between the two dragons.

the lights and noises heard coming from the University before the north went dark could have been:
- a confrontation between the faculty and Obsiduin
- an attempt by faculty to summon or find Obsiduin who was missing
whatever magic was involved, the spell went awry, causing everyone within the university grounds to be teleported north. they are frozen in time, encased in ice, currently under the watch of Ninguin.

Ninguin is lonely and has created a city made from ice, filled with creatures made from ice.

Obsiduin has done something similar in surrounding himself with Rosenrigg and the University, but remaining secret and isolated.

Both brothers struggle with being social but deeply desire social bonds and community.

Ninguin was responsible for the spell going awry. Obsiduin was visiting and the two were communicating (takes a long time to say) to try to resolve their differences and the headmaster and professors tried to summon Obsiduin back.

Ninguin tried to stop the magic and the spell backfired, teleporting the entire population of the University to his lair, and freezing them (space and time magic are related).

Obsiduin was also caught in the magic backfiring and is frozen in space/time. Ninguin is afraid to undo the magic because he is worried about Obsiduin's reaction. He thinks that Obsiduin will blame him and their relationship will deteriorate.

He doesn't know how to solve the situation so he is not doing anything.
