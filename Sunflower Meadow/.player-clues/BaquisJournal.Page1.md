**Last Entry from Baqui's Journal**

5th day of the 7th month of the year 482

Today marks an unforgettable chapter in our excavation of Barstrigg, a town that has captured the imagination of historians and archaeologists alike for its tragic end. Our focus shifted to a previously unexamined section of the ruins of the library. The site, nestled by the shimmering waters of the lake, offers a haunting beauty juxtaposed with the heavy sorrow of its past.

As I approached what remained of the library building, the remnants of its once-grand façade loomed above me, cloaked in ivy and moss. The structure had been built from local stone, now fractured and worn yet still exuding a sense of dignity. It was as if it stood at attention, silently mourning the lives it once housed within its walls.

The air was thick with the scent of damp earth and ancient wood. Shelves lay in disarray, some collapsed under their own weight, while others clung stubbornly to existence. Here, I discovered fragments of half-burned tomes, their spines warped and blackened, speaking volumes of the catastrophic day when everything changed.

The impact of the meteor had scorched the library, sealing it in a moment of chaos. Amidst the debris, I uncovered a small, intact book — a compendium of myths and folktales, its pages yellowed but delicate. It recounted tales of deities and creatures that once walked the shores of the lake, vibrant narratives that connected the people of Barstrigg to their land. Unfortunately, we found no sign of the old spell tome, not even a page containing a scrap of the spell we will need to locate Soliduin.

Tomorrow, we will continue our exploration in hopes of revealing more secrets hidden beneath layers of ash and rubble. This library, albeit in ruins, reaches across time to tell us its story and hopefully, to share the secrets of its long forgotten spells.
