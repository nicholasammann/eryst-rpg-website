**First Page of Baqui's Complete Histories**

The celestial seer, Celeduin, whose visions were as bright and pure as starlight, was the only one who truly understood the impending doom. Three days before the impact, a meteor, a fiery harbinger of destruction, streaked across the heavens. Celeduin, channeling the ethereal energy of the cosmos, warned of Barstrigg's annihilation. They pleaded with Soliduin, who possessed the potent fire magic necessary to deflect the celestial body, and Ninguin, whose mastery over ice could potentially slow the meteor's descent.

Celeduin proposed a combined assault: Ninguin would create a colossal ice barrier to cushion the impact, while Soliduin would use her flames to alter the meteor's trajectory. But Soliduin, convinced the meteor held untold riches, refused to share her power unless she was granted ownership of any precious metals that the meteor held within. Celeduin agreed to this request, but Ninguin rejected the proposal, arguing that any possible riches should be split evenly between the three. Celeduin, unable to mediate a solution between them, attempted a desperate solo effort; summoning a celestial shield, they tried to deflect the meteor themself.

The meteor, a behemoth of cosmic fire, struck the town. Soliduin, in a fit of avarice, scrambled to salvage what she could from the burning ruins, her flames adding to the inferno. Ninguin, untouched by the devastation, retreated to his ice caves in the far north. The celestial shield, a shimmering testament to Celeduin’s sacrifice, shattered, its light extinguished.

Barstrigg lay in ashes, a testament to the bitter feud that cost them everything and claimed the life of one who wished only to protect the people of the town.
