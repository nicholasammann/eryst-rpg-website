## The Wagon 

### Cast

3 siblings
- Nina (polar bear)
- Celine (panda bear)
- Solomon (black bear)

Wagon

Tent 1

Tent 2


### Scene 1 - the wagon hits camp

*Director: At the top of a hill, a large wagon speeds along a narrow road, soon to reach the downhill section of path that leads directly to a small camp at the base of the hill.*

**Wagon**: "Hum, dum, roll, roll! I'm off to see new lands! I will follow this road wherever it goes!"

*Director: In the camp, three bears sit by the fire: a polar bear, a panda bear, and a black bear. The panda bear looks up at the top of the hill.*

**Celine**: "Nina! Solomon! What is that in the distance, so late at night?"

*Director: The panda bear pulls a spyglass from their bag and looks at the wagon.*

**Celine**: "Oh no! Siblings! That wagon is driving far too quickly for such a small road."

*Director: The panda bear, Celine, hands the spyglass to the black bear.*

**Solomon**: "I see it too... it is certainly traveling far too enthusiastically for this path. The nerve of some wagons!"

**Wagon**: "What lies ahead? Oh, I must know! No time to waste, even faster I must go! Faster, faster!"

**Solomon**: "What is this I spy? A trove of goods, in the back of the wagon? With those goods, I could surely found my university."

*Director: The polar bear, Nina, grabs the spyglass from Solomon.*

**Nina**: "That wagon will surely come this way! There is no chance it will slow down enough to turn aside from our camp!"

**Celine**: "If we work together, we can turn it aside. Let us plan, quickly! We can't let the wagon destroy our tents!"

**Tent 1**: "Forsooth, my woes are beyond counting. Oh, to be a tent in a cruel, harsh world!"

**Tent 2**: "Noble bears, do not leave! We are but tents, and we cannot flee!"

**Tent 1**: "What a terrible day, yet it must be fate... a wagon, traveling on this path so late?"

**Solomon**: "I will help, but only if I can have the goods in the back of this wagon."

**Celine**: "Fine, I agree, now let us hurry! It's coming down the hill!"

*Director: The wagon has started down the hill and is picking up even more speed.*

**Wagon**: "Roll, roll, on we go! There must be something MARVELOUS at the end of this road!"

*Director: Nina looks at Solomon.*

**Nina**: "Wait, why does Solomon get all of the goods? We should split the goods between us."

**Solomon**: "A third of the goods is not enough - I need them all!"

*Director: Celine hurries a short way up the road and begins to pile branches and small logs into a makeshift barricade.*

**Nina**: "You can't have them all! I want at least a third! Why do you always have to be this way?"

*Director: The wagon has almost reached the bottom of the hill, and is going at a frightful speed.*

**Wagon**: "Mother, father, look at me fly! A wagon has never flown oh so high!"

**Tent 2**: "Oh, bears, watch out!"

**Tent 1**: "Perilous is the life of a tent... I knew it would come to this."

**Wagon**: "Hello, hello! Oh my, oh no!"

*Director: The wagon crashes through the barricade, slowing down considerably but not enough. Celine stands on the road with paws outstretched...*

**Nina**: "Celine! No!"



### Scene 2 - Nina and Solomon mourn Celine, blame each other

*Director: The wagon lays on its side in the middle of camp. The tents lay in a heap. Nina and Solomon sit at the body of their fallen sibling in the road.*

**Tent 2**: "Oh, brave Celine... to have spent your life so selflessly..."

**Tent 1**: "A tragedy beyond what even I imagined... we tents will not forget you."

*Director: Nina and Solomon began to talk in increasingly raised voices.*

**Nina**: "This is your fault! Celine was struck down by your greed as much as by any wagon!"

**Solomon**: "How dare you! Who was it that demanded a third of the goods? Not Celine! Only you!"

*Director: Nina and Solomon both stand up, pointing paws at each other.*

**Solomon**: "You will never change!"

**Nina**: "Because I don't need to! Take the goods, if that is what you care about. I will bury Celine on my own. I don't need your help - I never have."

*Director: Solomon walks back to the camp. Nina cries at Celine's side.*


### Scene 3 - the tents mourn Celine

*Director: The goods are gone, and Solomon as well. The wagon lies to the side of the camp, and in the center of the ruined tents sits Nina, beside a large stone.*

**Nina**: "Celine - I shall miss you always. Curse Solomon... and yet, I miss him as well. But there can be no mending these broken ties - not yours, not mine."

**Tent 1**: "No day has been so dark... and now, I see the darkness approach. A tent was never meant to live forever, I suppose."

**Tent 2**: "Here you shall stand forever, noble bear. And we will stay, to watch over your monument standing there."

*Director: Nina walks away north, leaving the camp and the stone monument behind.*

**Wagon**: "Only stillness, where the wind in my wheels once flowed... it seems, this is the end of the road."
